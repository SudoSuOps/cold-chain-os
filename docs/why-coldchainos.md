# Why coldchainOS

> Does the cold chain pencil?
> The product has a clock. The building has a power bill. The deal has assumptions. Connect them.

coldchainOS is an independent, self-funded, open-source project in active development. The founder brings 30 years of commercial real estate transaction experience and prior national industrial team leadership. No venture capital. No token. We are paying practical experience forward and inviting useful collaboration.

**Status.** There is no finished platform, no customers, no third-party integrations, no measured savings and no certifications. This repository contains a website, an educational calculator, illustrative worked examples, a sample of a CRE question-and-answer dataset, and a local-only prototype. Everything else here is direction.

## The problem

Cold storage is, at once, a building, a refrigeration system, an electrical load, a logistics node, a place where valuable time-sensitive inventory sits, an operating business (or a facility supporting one), and a capital decision.

Different documents describe different pieces:

| Record | What it describes |
|---|---|
| Offering memorandum | The building, as marketed |
| Utility bills | Part of the energy economics, for specific periods |
| Service records | Equipment history |
| Lease | Allocation of certain costs and responsibilities |
| Inventory records | Product movement |
| Lender package | A different bundle of information again |

They can conflict, omit important details, or describe different periods and operating conditions. Decisions move forward while critical assumptions stay disconnected from their evidence.

## What we intend to contribute

1. **Make the source visible.** Every figure links back to where it came from.
2. **Make missing information explicit.** A gap is a finding, not something to smooth over.
3. **Make the calculation reproducible.** Inputs, formulas and units are on the page.
4. **Keep consequential decisions with accountable people.** Tools assemble and check evidence; people decide.

## How to read the site

Every statement is one of four kinds, and the site says which: **documented public evidence** (with source, date and scope), **illustrative** scenarios, **founder-reported** project facts, or **planned** capabilities. See `sources.json`, `market-context.md`, `illustrative-examples.md` and `nda-data-handling.md`.
