# Market context

This document explains scale without inventing a market size. As of 2026-11-09. Sources and qualifications are in `sources.json`.

## Three kinds of "velocity" (not interchangeable)

- **Product velocity:** how fast inventory moves through a facility. Measures: pallets received and shipped per day, average dwell time, inventory turns, dock turnaround, throughput per available pallet position.
- **Property market velocity:** how fast facilities lease or transact. Measures: days on market, leasing absorption, sales activity, vacancy, time from offer to closing.
- **Information velocity:** how fast a team can assemble, verify and review decision evidence. Measures: time to collect required documents, time to identify contradictions, unresolved questions, time to produce a source-linked brief.

High occupancy does not mean high throughput. High throughput does not mean high profit. Faster review does not mean less diligence.

**Hypothesis (not a result):** better-organized evidence may reduce avoidable review delays. We have not measured this.

We do not publish a TAM. Food and pharmaceutical cold chains are related but distinct: temperature requirements, validation procedures, regulation and practice differ and are not interchangeable.

## One documented figure, with its limits

UNEP and FAO's 2022 report, *Sustainable Food Cold Chains: Opportunities, Challenges and the Way Forward*, cites an estimate that 526 million tons of food production (12% of the global total) was lost in 2017 due to lack of effective refrigeration. That is a 2017, global, estimated figure (UNEP's Ozone Secretariat attributes it to an International Institute of Refrigeration 2021 source). It is not a 2026 measurement and not an addressable market. We have not read the report itself (its pages block automated access); see `sources.json`.

## One current property-market data point

Newmark reported U.S. cold-storage vacancy of 7.7% in the first half of 2026, alongside the sector's first negative first-half net absorption since 2007 (published Aug 27, 2026). National figures only. The same report cites about 41 million cubic feet of deliveries; we keep supply additions and changes in occupied inventory separate and do not publish a net-absorption magnitude until the underlying table is confirmed.

## Practical value we can describe honestly

Earlier identification of missing evidence; more consistent review; transparent operating-cost scenarios; clearer separation of property and operating-business economics; better-prepared questions for engineers, operators, lenders and counsel. None is a measured result.

## Buildings and operators (dated, regional or member-limited)

- **CBRE, Midwest Cold Storage Trends 2025** (published 2025-03-31; market table Q4 2024; PDF pages noted): Midwest cold-storage space estimated at 39.5 million sq. ft., 56% built before 1990, and renovations that can make older facilities uneconomic to reuse (p. 4). Of about 1.5 million sq. ft. delivered since the start of 2023, 89% was build-to-suit (p. 5). New refrigerated warehouse construction averages $240 to $350 per sq. ft. nationally, and power is increasingly difficult to acquire amid data-center development (p. 6). These are regional, dated estimates, not current national vacancy, and the construction cost is for new buildings, not renovation.
- **GCCA 2026 Global Top 25** (2026-04-29): member operators run 7.76 billion cubic feet, up 6.3% on 2025, led by Lineage, Americold, NewCold, United States Cold Storage and Emergent Cold Latin America. Members only: not total worldwide capacity, not occupied inventory. The PDF's rows sum to about 7.75 billion, and year-over-year growth compares different company sets.
- **GCCA Cold Chain Index**: five expense classes (labor, electric power, supplies, repairs, rent); detailed values are member-only. Used here only as a structure for operating-cost assumptions.
- **GCCA 2026 Americas Cold Storage Capacity Report**: exists (listing dated 2026-08-31) but is member-only; contents unverified and unused.

The question these support: *what does this specific facility need to operate, and does that pencil?*
